## 2. Purpose and Scope

This procedure provides the synthetic controlled workflow for **Hydraulic Pressure Sensor Verification and Calibration**.

## 3. Applicable Machine Models

- MX-200
- MX-220
- MX-300

## 4. Applicable Components

- Hydraulic Pressure Sensor

## 5. Prerequisites

- Use a calibrated service pressure gauge suitable for the machine pressure range.
- Confirm that the technician is authorized for hydraulic and control-system work.

## 6. Safety Warnings

- **WARNING:** Do not exceed the machine model's maximum approved pressure.
- **WARNING:** Stop the procedure if unstable pressure or leakage is observed.

## 7. Procedure

1. Review active sensor-related alarms and previous calibration records.
2. Inspect the sensor connector and wiring.
3. Connect the approved reference gauge at the designated service point.
4. Compare the machine sensor value with the reference gauge at defined pressure points.
5. Record the deviation at each pressure point.
6. Adjust calibration only through the approved service interface.
7. Repeat the comparison and confirm the permitted tolerance.
8. Replace the sensor when calibration cannot achieve the permitted tolerance.
9. Record the final result and the reference instrument used.
