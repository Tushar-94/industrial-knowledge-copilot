## 2. Purpose and Scope

This procedure provides the synthetic controlled workflow for **Hydraulic Return Filter Replacement**.

## 3. Applicable Machine Models

- MX-200
- MX-220
- MX-300

## 4. Applicable Components

- Hydraulic Return-Line Filter

## 5. Prerequisites

- Confirm the approved replacement part for the machine model.
- Complete SOP-HSE-001 before opening the filter housing.

## 6. Safety Warnings

- **WARNING:** Do not open the housing until residual pressure has been verified as released.
- **WARNING:** Prevent contamination from entering the hydraulic circuit.

## 7. Procedure

1. Confirm the machine model and approved filter part number.
2. Complete hazardous-energy isolation and pressure-release verification.
3. Clean the exterior of the filter housing.
4. Position an approved container for residual hydraulic fluid.
5. Open the housing using the approved service tool.
6. Remove the used filter element and inspect it for abnormal contamination.
7. Inspect the housing and sealing surfaces.
8. Install the approved replacement element and seal.
9. Close the housing to the approved service specification.
10. Restore the system under the controlled recommissioning process.
11. Inspect for leakage and verify normal differential pressure.
12. Record the replacement in the maintenance system.
