## 2. Purpose and Scope

This procedure provides the synthetic controlled workflow for **Normal Machine Startup and Pre-Start Checks**.

## 3. Applicable Machine Models

- MX-200
- MX-220
- MX-300

## 4. Applicable Components

- Safety Light Curtain
- Main Electric Motor
- Hydraulic Reservoir

## 5. Prerequisites

- The operator must be trained and authorized for the machine model.
- No active maintenance isolation may remain on the machine.

## 6. Safety Warnings

- **WARNING:** Do not bypass guards, interlocks, or protective devices.
- **WARNING:** Do not start the machine while a critical alarm remains active.

## 7. Procedure

1. Inspect the work area and remove unauthorized objects.
2. Confirm guards and access doors are closed.
3. Verify that emergency-stop devices are released.
4. Verify that the light curtain and safety circuit report ready.
5. Inspect the hydraulic-fluid level.
6. Enable control power and review the alarm display.
7. Start the hydraulic power unit.
8. Verify stable pressure, normal motor current, and acceptable oil temperature.
9. Perform the approved no-load functional cycle.
10. Release the machine for production only when all checks pass.
