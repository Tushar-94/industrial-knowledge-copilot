## 5. Prerequisites

- The operator must understand the alarm-severity classification.

## 6. Safety Warnings

- **WARNING:** Critical safety alarms must never be bypassed.
- **WARNING:** Repeated restart attempts are prohibited after thermal or safety trips.

## 7. Procedure

1. Stop material feeding and complete the current safe cycle when permitted.
2. Move the machine to the approved safe position.
3. Stop automatic operation.
4. Stop the hydraulic power unit when required by the alarm response.
5. Record the active alarm code and machine operating state.
6. Escalate high and critical alarms to authorized maintenance personnel.
7. Apply hazardous-energy isolation when maintenance work is required.
8. Do not restart until the fault has been corrected and release has been authorized.
