## 2. Purpose and Scope

This procedure provides the synthetic controlled workflow for **Hazardous Energy Isolation for Press Maintenance**.

## 3. Applicable Machine Models

- MX-200
- MX-220
- MX-300

## 4. Applicable Components

- This procedure applies at machine level rather than to one specific component.

## 5. Prerequisites

- Work must be performed by personnel authorized under the site energy-control program.
- The machine-specific isolation points must be identified before work begins.

## 6. Safety Warnings

- **WARNING:** This synthetic procedure is for demonstration only and is not approved for real equipment.
- **WARNING:** Control-panel stop commands do not replace physical hazardous-energy isolation.
- **WARNING:** Stored hydraulic pressure must be released and verified before intrusive maintenance.

## 7. Procedure

1. Notify affected personnel that maintenance isolation is required.
2. Stop the machine using the approved controlled-shutdown procedure.
3. Isolate the approved electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, and stored-energy sources.
4. Apply personal lockout and identification devices according to the site program.
5. Release or restrain stored hydraulic and mechanical energy.
6. Verify isolation using the approved zero-energy verification method.
7. Perform the authorized maintenance task.
8. Inspect the work area and confirm that tools and personnel are clear.
9. Remove lockout devices only under the approved release process.
10. Restore energy in a controlled sequence and notify affected personnel.
