## 2. Purpose and Scope

This procedure provides the synthetic controlled workflow for **Hydraulic System Inspection**.

## 3. Applicable Machine Models

- MX-200
- MX-220
- MX-300

## 4. Applicable Components

- Hydraulic Return-Line Filter
- Hydraulic Pump
- Hydraulic Pressure Sensor
- Hydraulic Pressure Relief Valve
- Hydraulic Reservoir
- Hydraulic Fluid
- Hydraulic Oil Cooler

## 5. Prerequisites

- Review active alarms and recent maintenance history.
- Use the required personal protective equipment for the approved inspection scope.

## 6. Safety Warnings

- **WARNING:** Do not open pressurized hydraulic components.
- **WARNING:** Escalate visible high-pressure leakage immediately.

## 7. Procedure

1. Review the machine operating-hour count and maintenance schedule.
2. Inspect the reservoir level and visible hydraulic-fluid condition.
3. Inspect hoses, fittings, and accessible connections for leakage.
4. Record hydraulic pressure and oil temperature under the approved operating state.
5. Check return-filter differential pressure.
6. Listen for abnormal pump noise and inspect for pressure instability.
7. Inspect the oil cooler and verify cooling-flow condition.
8. Record findings and create corrective work where required.
