## 8. Hydraulic Return-Line Filter

Removes particulate contamination from hydraulic fluid returning to the reservoir.

### Maintenance requirements

**Inspect:** 500 operating hours.

Investigate the filter when differential pressure reaches 1.0 bar.

**Related procedure:** SOP-MNT-001 — Hydraulic System Inspection

**Replace:** 1,000 operating hours or 12 months, whichever occurs first.

Replace the filter earlier when differential pressure reaches 1.5 bar.

**Related procedure:** SOP-MNT-002 — Hydraulic Return Filter Replacement


## 9. Hydraulic Fluid

Transfers energy, lubricates components, and removes heat from the hydraulic system.

### Maintenance requirements

**Sample:** 1,000 operating hours or 12 months, whichever occurs first.

No additional condition-based trigger is defined.

**Related procedure:** SOP-MNT-001 — Hydraulic System Inspection

**Replace:** 4,000 operating hours or 36 months, whichever occurs first.

Replace the hydraulic fluid earlier when laboratory analysis shows that the approved fluid condition limits are no longer met.

**Related procedure:** SOP-MNT-001 — Hydraulic System Inspection


## 10. Hydraulic Pump

Supplies hydraulic flow and pressure to the forming system.

### Maintenance requirements

**Performance Inspection:** 2,000 operating hours or 24 months, whichever occurs first.

Inspect the pump earlier when abnormal noise or unstable hydraulic pressure is observed.

**Related procedure:** SOP-MNT-001 — Hydraulic System Inspection


## 11. Hydraulic Pressure Sensor

Measures hydraulic operating pressure for control and diagnostic functions.

### Maintenance requirements

**Calibration Check:** 2,000 operating hours or 24 months, whichever occurs first.

Perform a calibration check when sensor deviation exceeds 3 bar compared with the approved reference gauge.

**Related procedure:** SOP-MNT-003 — Hydraulic Pressure Sensor Verification and Calibration


## 12. Pressure Relief Valve

Limits hydraulic-system pressure by diverting excess flow.

### Maintenance requirements

**Functional Inspection:** 1,000 operating hours or 12 months, whichever occurs first.

Inspect the relief valve when unstable pressure control is observed.

**Related procedure:** SOP-MNT-001 — Hydraulic System Inspection


## 13. Hydraulic Oil Cooler

Removes excess heat from hydraulic fluid during machine operation.

### Maintenance requirements

**Clean And Inspect:** 1,000 operating hours or 12 months, whichever occurs first.

Inspect the oil cooler when hydraulic-fluid temperature reaches 70 °C or higher.

**Related procedure:** SOP-MNT-001 — Hydraulic System Inspection


## 14. Safety Light Curtain

Detects entry into the protected operating area and prevents hazardous machine motion.

### Maintenance requirements

**Functional Test:** 500 operating hours or 6 months, whichever occurs first.

No additional condition-based trigger is defined.

**Related procedure:** SOP-OPS-001 — Normal Machine Startup and Pre-Start Checks


## 15. Guide Lubrication System

Delivers lubricant to press-guide surfaces to reduce friction and wear.

### Maintenance requirements

**Inspect:** 250 operating hours or 3 months, whichever occurs first.

Inspect the guide lubrication system when increased friction or abnormal guide noise is observed.

**Related procedure:** SOP-MNT-001 — Hydraulic System Inspection
